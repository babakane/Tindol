What is the Expert Program?

A certification program for verified professionals who want to maximize their impact and technical authority in the Tindol ecosystem.

The Difference

While all writers on Tindol build reputation, Certified Experts are vetted professionals with proven track records. They receive:

  • Verified badge on all content and profile
  • Highest authority potential and platform leadership
  • Priority access to research collaboration
  • Featured placement on homepage and search
  • Dedicated account manager and support

Requirements & Process

We maintain high standards to ensure our readers receive the best expertise available.

Requirements by Tier

Certified Expert

  • 5+ years of professional experience
  • Published 3+ technical articles (any platform)
  • Active GitHub/GitLab profile with quality projects
  • Pass technical assessment in your domain
  • Complete one trial project for Tindol

Senior Expert

  • 8+ years of professional experience
  • Published 10+ articles or 1 book/major publication
  • Speaking experience (conferences, meetups)
  • Enterprise-scale project experience
  • 1+ years as Certified Expert on Tindol

Distinguished Expert

  • 12+ years of professional experience
  • Recognized industry authority
  • Multiple conference keynotes or published books
  • Enterprise consulting experience at scale
  • Invited by Tindol editorial board
